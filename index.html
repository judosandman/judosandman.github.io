A fast word game for iPhone and iPad. Spell a word to blast each row before the clock runs out, chain quick words into a Genius Combo, and race a friend in multiplayer.

Support: vincentbong470@gmail.com

Help

  • Restoring purchases. On a new device or after reinstalling, open the shop, choose GET COINS and tap Restore Purchases. Remove Ads and Double Coins come back. Coin packs are used up when you spend them, so they cannot be restored.
  • Multiplayer. Both players need to be signed in to Game Center (Settings > Game Center), with different accounts.
  • Ads. An ad plays after every second game over in single-player and after every multiplayer match. Remove Ads in the shop turns them off for good. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. To report an inappropriate ad, tap the AdChoices icon on the ad, or email us at the address above.
  • Privacy choices. You can change tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, you can change your ad consent with Privacy Settings at the bottom of the main menu.
