TYPE SHAPE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: Type Shape has no accounts and no servers of its own. We, the developer, do not collect your personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. Apple runs Game Center and in-app purchases. Google provides the ads, and Google's ad software collects some data to show and measure them. You choose whether you can be tracked and, where the law requires it, whether ads can be personalised.

Who we are

Type Shape is a word game for iPhone and iPad. "We" and "us" in this policy mean the developer of Type Shape. If you have any question about this policy or your information, email vincentbong470@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Type Shape saves your game on your device, and it is never sent to us. The save holds:

  • your coins, power-ups and purchases
  • your best score and your ten best runs (score, rows, shapes and date)
  • your settings, such as theme and sound
  • whether you have finished the tutorial
  • a count of games used to space out ads
  • the transaction numbers of coin purchases, so a purchase is never credited twice

Google's ad and consent software also stores your consent choice and some of its own ad data on your device.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. You can restore Remove Ads and Double Coins with Restore Purchases on the GET COINS tab of the shop.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, Type Shape uses your Game Center player ID and name to send the score of each single-player game to the leaderboard, where Game Center keeps your best, and to show the leaderboard.

In multiplayer matches, game data passes between you and your opponent through Game Center. That data includes your Game Center name, which your opponent sees, the letters and words you type, your scores and match messages.

If a new best score cannot be sent right away, because you are offline or not signed in, the app keeps it on your device until it can be sent. If you were signed in, it also keeps your Game Center player ID with it. A best score set while signed out is sent the next time a Game Center player signs in on the device.

Apple handles all Game Center data under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store it. You can manage Game Center in your device's Settings.

In-app purchases (Apple)

The App Store processes all payments. We never see your name, payment card or other payment details. The app only receives confirmation of what was bought, so it can unlock it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Type Shape is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. They appear between games: after every second game over in single-player, and after every multiplayer match. Google's ad software starts when you open the app and loads an ad in advance, so it can collect information even before an ad is shown.

When ads are served, Google and its advertising partners (third-party vendors, such as advertisers and the ad technology companies that bid on and measure ads) may place and read cookies, use web beacons and similar technologies, and use your IP address and device identifiers to collect information from the app. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, this information may include:

  • Device identifiers. Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking. Google may also use identifiers that are limited to this app or developer.
  • IP address. This is used to estimate your general location, such as your country or city. It is not used for your precise location.
  • Ad and app interaction data. This includes the ads you see and tap, video views, and how you interact with the app, such as when you open it.
  • Diagnostics. This includes crash logs and performance data, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use, which may also be used for advertising and measurement.

Google's consent software in the app also uses your approximate location (from your IP address) and basic performance and usage data to work out whether consent rules apply to you.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, detect fraud and improve its services. If you allow it, Google also uses it to show ads more relevant to you. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Type Shape asks Google to show only ads suitable for general audiences. If you see an ad you think is inappropriate, tap the AdChoices icon on the ad or email us.

Your choices

  • Tracking. When the first ad is due, iOS asks whether Type Shape may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared, and under Apple's rules Google may not use data from this app to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent in the UK, the EEA and Switzerland. If you are in one of these regions, the app asks for your choices through Google's consent form before any ads are requested. The form lists Google and the ad partners that may use your information, and what they use it for (see Manage options). If you do not consent, you may still see limited, non-personalised ads. While ads are on, you can change your choice at any time with Privacy Settings at the bottom of the main menu.
  • Remove Ads. The Remove Ads purchase turns ads off for good. From then on, the app does not start Google's ad or consent software.

Children

Type Shape is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep no copy of your information, because the app has no servers. Data on your device is deleted when you delete the app. Google keeps advertising data under its own policies, and Apple keeps Game Center and App Store data under its own. You can manage both in your device's Settings and your Apple and Google accounts.

Any third party that receives user data through Type Shape, namely Google and its advertising partners for ads and consent, and Apple for Game Center and the App Store, provides the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this policy and as required by Apple's App Store Review Guidelines.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. Because we hold none of it, most requests are handled through Apple or Google, using the links above. Email us if you need help, and we will point you to the right place.
