TYPE SHAPE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: Type Shape has no accounts. We, the developer, keep no personal information about you apart from any email you send us, and your game progress is saved on your device. On iPhone and iPad, Apple runs Game Center and in-app purchases. On Android, Google runs Google Play Games and Google Play purchases, and multiplayer matches pass through our own match server, which keeps nothing once a match ends. Google provides the ads, and Google's ad software collects some data to show and measure them. On iPhone and iPad you choose whether you can be tracked, on Android you control your device's advertising ID, and, where the law requires it, you choose whether ads can be personalised.

Who we are

Type Shape is a word game for iPhone, iPad and Android. "We" and "us" in this policy mean the developer of Type Shape. If you have any question about this policy or your information, email vincentbong470@gmail.com.

What is saved on your device

Type Shape saves your game on your device. The save itself is never sent to us. On Android, the multiplayer name described under Type Shape on Android goes to our match server when you play a match. The save holds:

  • your coins, power-ups and purchases
  • your best score and your ten best runs (score, rows, shapes and date)
  • your settings, such as theme and sound
  • whether you have finished the tutorial
  • a count of games used to space out ads
  • the transaction numbers of coin purchases (on Android, Google Play's purchase tokens), so a purchase is never credited twice
  • a best score that is waiting to be sent to the leaderboard, with your player ID if you were signed in
  • the time your last multiplayer match started
  • on Android, the multiplayer name the app makes up for you (such as "User4821")

Google's ad and consent software also stores your consent choice and some of its own ad data on your device.

Deleting the app deletes all of this from your device. A copy may remain in a backup of your device: an iCloud or computer backup on iPhone and iPad, or your Google backup on Android (see Android backup). You can restore Remove Ads and Double Coins with Restore Purchases on the GET COINS tab of the shop.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

On iPhone and iPad, if you are signed in to Game Center, Type Shape uses your Game Center player ID and name to send the score of each single-player game to the leaderboard, where Game Center keeps your best, and to show the leaderboard.

Multiplayer matches on iPhone and iPad run on Game Center: game data passes between you and your opponent through Game Center. That data includes your Game Center name, which your opponent sees, the letters and words you type, your scores and match messages.

If a new best score cannot be sent right away, because you are offline or not signed in, the app keeps it on your device until it can be sent. If you were signed in, it also keeps your Game Center player ID with it. A best score set while signed out is sent the next time a Game Center player signs in on the device.

Apple handles all Game Center data under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store it. You can manage Game Center in your device's Settings.

In-app purchases (iPhone and iPad)

On iPhone and iPad, the App Store processes all payments. We never see your name, payment card or other payment details. The app only receives confirmation of what was bought, so it can unlock it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Type Shape is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. They appear between games: after every second game over in single-player, and after every multiplayer match. Google's ad software starts when you open the app and loads an ad in advance, so it can collect information even before an ad is shown.

When ads are served, Google and its advertising partners (third-party vendors, such as advertisers and the ad technology companies that bid on and measure ads) may place and read cookies, use web beacons and similar technologies, and use your IP address and device identifiers to collect information from the app. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, this information may include:

  • Device identifiers. On iPhone and iPad, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking. On Android, your device's advertising ID is used unless you have deleted it, along with the app set ID and, where they apply, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device. Google may also use identifiers that are limited to this app or developer.
  • IP address. This is used to estimate your general location, such as your country or city. It is not used for your precise location.
  • Ad and app interaction data. This includes the ads you see and tap, video views, and how you interact with the app, such as when you open it.
  • Diagnostics. This includes performance data, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use, which may also be used for advertising and measurement. On iPhone and iPad, it also includes crash logs.

Google's consent software in the app also uses your approximate location (from your IP address) and basic performance and usage data to work out whether consent rules apply to you.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, detect fraud and improve its services. If you allow it, Google also uses it to show ads more relevant to you. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Type Shape asks Google to show only ads suitable for general audiences. If you see an ad you think is inappropriate, tap the AdChoices icon on the ad or email us.

Your choices

  • Tracking (iPhone and iPad). The first time you open Type Shape, iOS asks whether it may track you. In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland, it asks only after you consent in Google's form below, and not at all if you do not. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared, and under Apple's rules Google may not use data from this app to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Advertising ID (Android). You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings, under Privacy > Ads on most devices. Once it is deleted, apps receive no advertising ID.
  • Consent in the UK, the EEA and Switzerland. If you are in one of these regions, the app asks for your choices through Google's consent form before any ads are requested. On iPhone and iPad this form comes first, before iOS asks whether the app may track you, and the form does not come up again by itself once you have answered iOS. The form lists Google and the ad partners that may use your information, and what they use it for (see Manage options). If you do not consent, you may still see limited, non-personalised ads. While ads are on, you can change your choice at any time with Privacy Settings at the bottom of the main menu.
  • Remove Ads. The Remove Ads purchase turns ads off for good. From then on, the app does not start Google's ad or consent software.

Type Shape on Android

Type Shape is also available for Android phones and tablets on Google Play. The sections above apply to both apps unless they say otherwise. The Android app differs in these ways:

  • Google Play Games instead of Game Center. The Google Play Games software starts each time you open the app, whether or not you sign in, and Google collects some information through it, such as your Play Games profile, how you use the game and data about how its software performs. When the app opens, Play Games may sign you in automatically. If it does not, the app asks you to sign in once each time it starts, and you can also sign in with SIGN IN on the leaderboard. If you are signed in, Type Shape uses your Play Games player ID and name to send the score of each single-player game to the High Score leaderboard, where Play Games keeps your best, and to show the leaderboard. Other players can see your name and score there, depending on your Play Games profile settings. You can play without signing in. If a new best score cannot be sent right away, because you are offline or not signed in, the app keeps it on your device until it can be sent. If you were signed in, it also keeps your Play Games player ID with it. A best score set while signed out is sent the next time a Play Games player signs in on the device, and appears on the leaderboard under that player's name. We do not use Play Games data for advertising. Google handles all Play Games data under Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its game data in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.
  • Google Play purchases instead of the App Store. Google Play processes all payments. We never see your name, payment card or other payment details. The app only receives confirmation of what was bought, so it can unlock it, and keeps Google Play's purchase token on your device so a coin purchase is never credited twice. Google Play's billing software in the app also sends Google technical information about how it is working, such as error codes and your device model. Remove Ads and Double Coins come back by themselves on a device signed in to the same Google Account, or with Restore Purchases on the GET COINS tab of the shop.
  • Multiplayer through our match server. On Android, multiplayer matches run through Type Shape's own match server instead of Game Center. It pairs two players who are searching at the same time and passes game data between them. Your opponent is another Android player. The server receives the first 14 characters of your Google Play Games name (or, if you are not signed in or the app's name filter does not allow that name, a name such as "User4821" that the app makes up once and keeps on your device), the letters and words you type, your scores, and the game's own signals, such as when a word is claimed or the match ends. Like any internet server, it also receives your IP address, which it uses only to limit how many connections one address can open at once, not to find out where you are. The server keeps this information in memory only while you are searching for or playing a match. It does not write it to disk or to its logs, and it keeps nothing once you disconnect. The connection to it is encrypted. Your opponent sees your name (in capitals, up to 14 characters), your score and the letters you type while you play. There is no chat. The server is hosted for us by Render, which passes its connections through Cloudflare's network. Render and Cloudflare may each keep routine technical records such as connection logs, under Render's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • The advertising ID instead of the IDFA. Android does not ask whether the app may track you. Google's ad software uses your device's advertising ID, unless you have deleted it, and the app set ID, an identifier limited to apps from the same developer, together with the other information listed under Advertising above. On devices that offer Android's ad privacy features, it may also use Android's ad topics and ad measurement, which you can turn off in your device's ad privacy settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID as described under Your choices.
  • Android backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data, which is the save described under What is saved on your device and what Google's ad and consent software stores there, in its backup to your Google Account, and restore it when you install Type Shape again, including on a new device. Google keeps this backup under Google's Privacy Policy, and we cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your device's Settings.
  • Questions and deletion. See Deleting your data.

In the terms Google Play uses in the app's Data safety section, the Android app collects and shares: approximate location (estimated from your IP address, for ads); user IDs (your Play Games player ID and name, and the name sent to our match server); app interactions (how you use the app and its ads); other actions (the letters, words and scores in a match, which your opponent sees, and your leaderboard scores); diagnostics (performance data); and device or other IDs (the advertising ID, the app set ID and, where they apply, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device).

Children

Type Shape is not directed at children under 13, and its target audience on Google Play is ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Keeping data

We do not store your information on any server of ours. The iPhone and iPad app does not connect to any server of ours. On Android, our match server holds match information in memory only while you are searching for or playing a match, and keeps nothing once you disconnect. Render and Cloudflare, which host and carry our match server's connections, may keep routine connection logs, which can include your IP address, for a limited time under their own policies. If you email us, we keep your email address and message only as long as we need to answer you. Google keeps advertising, Google Play Games, Google Play and Android backup data under its own policies, and Apple keeps Game Center, App Store and iCloud backup data under its own. You can manage these in your device's Settings and your Apple and Google accounts.

Every third party that receives user data through Type Shape provides the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this policy and as required by Apple's App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play's policies. They are: Google and its advertising partners, for ads and consent; Apple, for Game Center and the App Store on iPhone and iPad; Google, for Google Play Games, Google Play and device backup on Android; and Render and Cloudflare, which host and carry our match server's connections on Android. In a multiplayer match, your opponent also sees your name, your score and the letters you type, as described above.

Deleting your data

Type Shape has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. To ask us to delete your data, or to ask what we hold, email vincentbong470@gmail.com with the subject "Type Shape data deletion" and say whether you play on Android or on iPhone or iPad. You do not need to send anything else. Our match server keeps nothing about you once a match ends, so apart from your email there is nothing on our side to delete, and we keep your email only as long as we need to answer you. We will reply and tell you how to delete what Google or Apple keeps. You can also delete it yourself:

  • The save on your device: uninstall Type Shape, or on Android clear its storage in your device's Settings (Apps > Type Shape > Storage on most devices).
  • Device backups: on Android, in your device's Settings or your Google Account; on iPhone and iPad, in your iCloud backup settings or on the computer that holds the backup.
  • Advertising identifier: on Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings (Privacy > Ads on most devices); on iPhone and iPad, turn off tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Google Play Games: manage or delete your Play Games profile and its game data in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.
  • Game Center and other Apple data: in Settings on your iPhone or iPad, or at privacy.apple.com.

Routine connection logs kept by Render and Cloudflare are handled under their own policies.

How we protect your information

The app's connections over the internet, including to our match server, Google and Apple, are encrypted. The Android app blocks unencrypted connections, and the iPhone and iPad app keeps Apple's default rules, which require encrypted ones. Our match server holds what it receives in memory only, while you are connected to it. It writes no names, words or IP addresses to disk or to its logs. It limits the size and rate of the messages it passes on, and how many connections one address can open. We do not use Google Play Games data or match data for advertising, and we pass none of it to advertisers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. Because we keep none of it apart from any email you send us, most requests are handled through Apple or Google, using the links above. Email us if you need help, and we will point you to the right place.
